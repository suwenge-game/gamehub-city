What this list is

Every entry below is a five-letter word that has appeared as a daily answer on this site. Feedback playbook for this pattern

Green-lock reading. Once this pattern is confirmed, treat the table above as the entire game: 246 candidates, S/T/C-leading in most cases, closing on S/E. Yellow handling inside the pattern.
