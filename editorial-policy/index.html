Editorial policy

Rules and guidance are checked against the live original game engine before publication.

Accuracy

We test examples against the same duplicate-letter evaluator used during play. Strategy guidance distinguishes reliable deductions from optional tactics.

Word selection

Answer candidates favor recognizable five-letter English words. A broader accepted-guess dictionary reduces unnecessary rejection while keeping every entry alphabetic and five letters long.

Corrections

Readers can report unclear rules, unsuitable words, or technical problems through the contact page. Material corrections receive a new visible update date.

Updates and fact checks

Mechanics claims are compared with the current interface and engine. Dates, links, examples, and storage descriptions are checked when a page changes. We avoid invented ratings, player totals, and claims that one opener is universally optimal.

AI assistance

Tools may assist drafting or code review. A human publisher remains responsible for the final site and correction decisions.

Editorial independence

Advertising does not determine answer selection, guide conclusions, rule explanations, or whether a correction is accepted. Generated text is not treated as a factual source; the publisher remains responsible for verification.
