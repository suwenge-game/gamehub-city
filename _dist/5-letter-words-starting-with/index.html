Why pattern pages, not word lists

Every page in the explorer is generated from the same answer bank the daily puzzle uses, and every statistic is computed, not quoted. A pattern page gives you three things a dictionary cannot: the exact membership of the pattern inside this game, the positional and vowel texture of that membership, and the tactics that follow from those numbers. When your board narrows to a pattern, the matching page is your candidate table.

The explorer grows with the bank — new top letters, suffixes, and prefixes are generated automatically as the answer list evolves, and stale patterns drop off when their membership falls below threshold. Nothing here is hand-curated, so nothing here drifts.
