Privacy policy

Your guesses, results, and streak statistics are stored locally in your browser and are not uploaded by the game.

Local game data

We use localStorage to resume the current daily puzzle and maintain played games, wins, streaks, and guess distribution. You can remove this data by clearing site storage.

Analytics and advertising

This site uses Google Analytics (analytics) and Google AdSense (advertising). Analytics cookies are set only after you grant "analytics" consent in our privacy-choices dialog; advertising cookies are written only after you grant "advertising" consent. Before you make a choice, no ad scripts are loaded and no ad or analytics cookies are written. Reopen the dialog any time with the "Privacy choices" button at the bottom-left of every page (choice stored locally, key gh_consent_v1). These services may process device, usage, cookie, or similar identifiers under their own policies.

Contact

If you contact the publisher, the information you provide is used to answer your message and manage the request.

Purpose and retention

Local data restores the board and calculates games, wins, streaks, and distribution. It remains until browser behavior or the player removes it. Contact messages are kept only as reasonably needed to answer and manage the issue.

Third-party processing

Google Analytics measures page usage only after "analytics" consent; Google AdSense supplies ads only after "advertising" consent. You can withdraw consent at any time via the "Privacy choices" button.

Your choices

You can block cookies through browser settings, clear localStorage, or use privacy controls offered by the relevant service.
