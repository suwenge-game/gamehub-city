Contact

For corrections, word feedback, accessibility issues, or technical problems, email hello@gamehub.city.

Help us investigate

Include the page address, device and browser, what you expected, and what happened. For daily puzzle issues, include the date but avoid sending the answer in a public message.

Privacy requests

Game statistics are stored locally, so the publisher cannot retrieve or delete them remotely. You can clear them through browser site-data settings.

Response scope

We prioritize reproducible bugs, factual corrections, accessibility barriers, and unsuitable answer words.

Choose the right details

For a rejected guess, provide the word and game mode. For color feedback, describe the guess and tile pattern without putting an active answer in the subject line. For accessibility feedback, name the input method or assistive technology involved.

Corrections and word feedback

Link to the statement that seems wrong. If an answer feels unsuitable, identify obscurity, spelling, a proper name, an abbreviation, or harmful meaning as the concern. Reports are checked against live game behavior and editorial policy.

What not to send

Never email passwords, payment information, government identifiers, or other sensitive data. The game has no player account, so support never needs a password or your local statistics file. A particular response time is not guaranteed.
